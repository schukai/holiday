# Contributing to holiday

Everyone may propose corrections and additions. This public repository contains
generated release data, so do not edit files under `dist/` directly. Instead,
open an issue or pull request that identifies the affected jurisdiction and
provides the evidence described below. Maintainers reproduce accepted changes
from the private, audited source repository.

## Evidence required for data contributions

For every fact or dataset you submit, provide:

- the country and exact legal jurisdiction;
- the official publisher, title and source URL;
- the statute, regulation or official government page that supports it;
- the date and time you accessed it;
- the exact licence name, version and URL, or the legal basis for public-domain
  or official-work status;
- the attribution text required by the source;
- a description of anything you copied, extracted, translated, normalised or
  otherwise changed; and
- all known restrictions, exceptions and third-party rights.

Do not submit data copied from Wikipedia, blogs, commercial holiday services or
other third-party aggregators. They may help locate an official source, but
they are not accepted as evidence. Do not submit confidential information,
personal data, credentials or material whose rights are unclear.

## Rights and licence certification

By submitting a contribution, you certify that:

1. you created the contribution yourself and have the right to submit it;
2. the source, licence, attribution and processing information you provide is
   complete and accurate to the best of your knowledge;
3. you grant the contribution irrevocably under the licence applicable to its
   destination: CC BY 4.0 for data and MIT for documentation or software;
4. schukai GmbH and downstream recipients may reproduce, verify, modify,
   combine, distribute and use the contribution commercially under that
   licence without additional permission or payment;
5. to the extent permitted by applicable law, you waive or agree not to assert
   moral rights or similar rights only insofar as necessary to exercise those
   licensed rights; and
6. the contribution and the public record associated with it may be retained
   indefinitely and redistributed with the project.

You keep ownership of your contribution; no copyright assignment to schukai
GmbH takes place. You also retain any rights that cannot legally be waived.
Submission does not transfer trademarks, patents or rights you do not own. A
contribution is not accepted merely because it was submitted; maintainers must
complete the source intake and licence review before it can enter a release.

Do not contribute copied or converted records from another holiday database,
even if that database appears to have an open licence. Contributions must be
limited to your own description of a correction and references to official
legal or government sources. Maintainers independently encode the resulting
facts in the project database.

For commits, add this line using your real name and an email address you are
authorised to use:

```text
Signed-off-by: Your Name <your-email@example.com>
```

The sign-off records your agreement with the Developer Certificate of Origin
1.1 (https://developercertificate.org/) and the additional data-specific terms
above. Use `git commit -s` to add it. Do not sign off for another person unless
you are legally authorised to do so.

## Review

Maintainers may reject or request more evidence for any contribution. Unclear
rights, missing provenance or an incompatible licence stop the intake. No
submission is incorporated until its evidence and processing chain are stored,
hashed and reviewed in accordance with the project's provenance policy.
